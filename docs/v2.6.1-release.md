# Football Tracker v2.6.1

## Features

- Team Detail now has Overview, Matches, Players and History. History shares one All/League/Cup/Champions filter across Honors, Records and Best XI.
- Honors shows championships, runner-up finishes and knockout final appearances, latest-first independently expandable seasons, and every finalized individual award kind found in canonical events. League runner-up finishes do not count as final appearances.
- Award kinds and totals are visible initially; player lists and individual award moments start closed. Open an award, then a player count to see compact Goal Types-style, wrapping 9px moment badges. Player names have a separate navigation action. Recipients sort by count, most recent award, then player ID.
- Team Records offers exactly goals, assists, appearances and goalkeeper clean sheets for a season or all time. Each has a top-three preview, shared ranks and a virtualized full list. Unused substitutes are excluded, and records have no award eligibility threshold.
- Team season Best XI uses existing REV13 statistics, actual positions, canonical 4-3-3 candidate ordering and at least `ceil(teamMatches * 0.4)` appearances. Vacant roles stay vacant. This read-only selection creates no official award and does not change Players → Latest XI.
- Records → Team → Honors & History exposes all 17 existing rankings in Championships (4), Runner-ups & Finals (7), and Best Season Records (6). Individual Awards adds the overall team total and expandable per-kind rankings, with dedicated virtual View All routes.
- Competition Awards, Season Recap, Records History, Player career Awards, team History and team award rankings retain all joint recipients. Competition and Recap provide an individual navigation action for every recipient.

## Official shared-award policy

Primary-stat ties now award every maximum recipient:

| Scope | Shared awards |
| --- | --- |
| League / Cup / Champions | Top Scorer, Assist King |
| Combined season | Season Top Scorer, Season Assist King |
| League | Golden Glove, based on individual clean sheets |

Each winner receives one stable official event and one award count. Historical awards use the team recorded at award time; transfers, releases and later unused bench registrations do not move those events. Team titles and runner-up finishes remain separate from individual awards.

Singular winner fields remain compatibility projections. Active award displays and publication use recipient arrays. Existing zero-maximum candidate eligibility is preserved, with no new positive-stat gate. Unfinished competition/month/round/season awards remain unpublished.

Award policy revision is now **2**, independently of rating revision **13**. Existing seasons recalculate from saved records; no raw matches, IndexedDB schema or stored award migration is required. Competition MVPs, season MVP, monthly/final/round MVPs, Cup/Champions/Season goalkeeper awards, official Best XI and Team of the Month retain their previous selection rules.

## Log Match and scrolling

Log Match uses its parent's single representative-position map for unused bench/squad display and ordering, falling back to registered positions only without match history. Actual participants retain match roles, tactical aliases and position changes. Starting XI, bench capacity, substitutions, saved lineups and historic logs are preserved.

The existing floating Scroll-to-Top control now also covers Team Overview/Matches/History, Home, Latest Changes, Combinations, League History, and all three competitions' Players pages. Existing route coverage remains. Team Players, Match Detail, Squad Import, Season Recap and New/Edit Match remain excluded. The single App-owned button uses `.app-content`, the existing 500px threshold, smooth scrolling and bottom safe-area placement; route/content resize and filter/disclosure mutations update visibility and observers disconnect on cleanup.

## Performance and compatibility

The implementation shares cached official events and competition status, builds one scoped event index for team History/rankings, and derives only the selected History menu. Records and XI scope caches are bounded; immutable source-array changes and rating/award revisions invalidate the corresponding results. Hidden full ranking DOM is not rendered, and the existing virtual list/pitch components are reused.

The synthetic benchmark in [performance/v2.6.1.json](performance/v2.6.1.json) covers 576 matches, 367 players and 16 teams. In this local run: initial Records 62.13ms, team XI 3.42ms, Honors 270.11ms, team award aggregation 0.39ms; 100 cached Records+XI+Honors lookup sets took 0.19ms and returned identical cached objects. These are local Node measurements, not mobile browser frame times.

History filters, menus, disclosures and expanded records survive detail navigation/Back. Legacy route state receives safe defaults. Player Detail retains its existing season-based scope, while the originating History All-Time selection and scroll position remain in navigation state.
