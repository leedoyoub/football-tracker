# Football Tracker v2.6.2

This release is limited to Individual Honors names, Teams Progress presentation and release metadata.

- Team History → Individual Honors prefers the saved `player.name`, then the existing full-name/historical-label fallbacks. Long names retain wrapping. Player navigation, award counts, ordering, badges and disclosure state are unchanged; other screens' naming rules are unchanged.
- Cup Progress shows current-stage completion per team: `S1 0/1`, `S1 1/1`, `F 0/1` or `FR 0/1` for a final replay. A team that played first stays at `1/1` until the canonical stage advances. `NS`, `Out S…`, `Out F` and `Champ` remain.
- Champions Progress shows actual completed team matches, starting at `R16 0/3` or `F 0/2`. A valid, resolved pairing immediately displays the winner's next round at zero and the loser's `Out` status, even before other pairings finish. Completed own matches alone never imply advancement. Existing legacy match quotas and final-replay rules remain.
- League stays at the existing `played/30`. The 4×4 grid, card dimensions, shared season selectors and Progress toggle remain. Progress values can wrap within narrow cards.
- Only `compactTeamCompetitionProgress` changes inside competition.ts. Tournament generation, winner decisions, match assignment, ratings, official awards, Best XI and saved data/schema are unchanged. No new cache or per-card engine calculation is added.
- package.json, package-lock.json and src/config.ts all use **2.6.2**; Home and service-worker versioning continue to use APP_VERSION.
