# v2.6.5 — Best XI and Awards integration

Previous version: 2.6.4. New version: 2.6.5. Awards policy revision: 3. Rating engine revision: 13 (unchanged).

## Official Best XI / MVP / Race

- First Half (League 1–15R), Second Half (16–30R), League, Cup, Champions and Season share official evaluation facts.
- Existing AWARD_433, ATT 3 / MID 3 / CB 2 / LB 1 / RB 1 / GK 1, deterministic ordering and duplicate prevention remain. No forced insertion or global optimizer was added.
- Each positive credited playing-time match contributes one Raw Rating to the arithmetic average, independently of position/minutes. Regulation credit remains capped at 90 minutes; saved stoppage-time events and REV13 remain intact.
- Official eligibility is 50% of available historical team minutes. Selection adds the specified participation-scaled League/knockout base. Season uses 0.70 League + 0.60 Champions + 0.30 Cup, with natural maximum 0.48.
- MVP is the highest-ranked actual selected XI player. Race and pitches display the raw average, while selection/ranking uses the full selection score.
- Half completion requires all 16 canonical teams and all 15 unique canonical round slots. Existing full competition/season completion and final replay resolution remain authoritative.

There are no saved transfer dates/contracts. Recorded historical registrations (including bench registrations) delimit evidenced team stints; uncertain gaps use the last evidenced team and the first evidenced team covers the leading unknown interval. Overlapping recorded round opportunities are counted once, while distinct dated delayed schedules remain separate. Bonus contributions are weighted by actual played minutes per represented team and cannot double a competition base. Current registration/position never replaces historical evidence.

Cup remains its existing two-team elimination-per-stage format. Stages 5–6 correspond to places 5–8 (quarter-final base); stage 7 to places 3–4 (semi-final base). Confirmed finalists have secured the runner-up base, not a prospective champion base. Champions likewise uses the existing resolved bracket.

## Team History

The exact saved modal kickoff formation is preserved (including 4-2-1-3 versus 4-3-3), with a validated kickoff snapshot used to recover missing metadata. Saved detailed slots/coordinates are reused; known saved legacy names use existing tactical definitions. Unknown shapes without evidence stay empty.

Eligibility is 30% of the selected team's recorded scope minutes. Scores use only that team's whole-match raw averages, without team bonuses. All main detailed roles are filled first in deterministic score order; unselected players may compete for secondary roles with at least 20% of their credited position minutes. LB/RB remain distinct, normalized CB/CAM aliases are reused, and unqualified slots stay vacant. The screen calls the same Pitch presentation as Team Players / Latest XI. It generates no official events.
