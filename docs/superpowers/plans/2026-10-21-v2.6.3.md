## Architecture and invariants

- Saved match kickoff formation is historical truth. Merge all middle numeric
  lines before counting formation frequency. Use canonical match chronology for
  equal counts. Never infer historical formation from the current team or final
  player positions; invalid or absent historical data has an explicit empty state.
- Team History has its own selector. Reuse cumulative award candidate scores,
  REV13, deterministic ranking and 40% eligibility. Classify players by summed
  credited GK/DEF/MID/FW minutes in the selected historical team scope.
- Supply explicit coordinates to the existing Pitch. Keep official awards and
  Match Log rendering defaults unchanged. Five-player rows alternate upper/lower
  positions; vacancies remain visible with an opt-in presentation setting.
- Reuse official season/event/statistics caches. Measure baseline and candidate
  changes with identical multi-season fixtures. Retain only demonstrated wins.
  Invalidate cached projections after source-array item replacement as well as
  normal immutable store updates; bound retained scope entries.
- Preserve v2.6.2 names and Progress, official selection/policies, storage schema,
  competition operations, navigation and PWA version management.
