- History records are exactly goals, assists, appearances and clean sheets. Only season Best XI, with ceil(team matches × 0.4) eligibility.
- Reuse compact Goal Types badge styles, existing pitch, ranking virtualization and navigation scroll restoration.
- Work in the user-authorized current checkout; no force push. User explicitly authorized planning through implementation, commit/main push and deployment verification in this turn.
