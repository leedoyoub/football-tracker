# Football Tracker v2.5.12

Release date: 2026-10-16 (Asia/Seoul).

## Implemented

- A shared, derived official-awards model supplies career Awards, player award Records, team historical Records and Records History. Events have stable identities, season/competition/period, actual historical team, historical position and finalized status. Nothing is persisted as a new trophy table.
- Monthly Player/Team awards retain the three-Matchday block and 40% eligibility; unfinished blocks are excluded. Career details use `Season 1-3` notation without MD ranges.
- League Player of the League retains its score. League Golden Glove ranks clean-sheet count, ratio, save percentage, GA/90, saves/90, GK minutes and stable player ID, without a minimum appearance gate.
- Cup and Champions Player of the Final use combined final records and the extracted canonical MOM comparator, including deterministic ties. Cup replay records and the two-game-per-side Champions final are included only after the competition resolves.
- Champions R16/QF/SF each publish Player of the Round and Team of the Round after every required series completes. Existing 40% eligibility and 4-3-3 selection remain. Final is excluded from Team of the Round.
- Integrated Goalkeeper of the Season uses GK-match average plus the same League/Cup/Champions coefficients as Player of the Season, retaining 40% actual GK appearances. The old Golden Glove property remains only as an API compatibility alias to this same result; it is never a second event/trophy.
- Player Detail shows all-career expandable award groups with counts, newest periods first, and separate Championships/Runner-up Finishes. Team honors require actual appearances for that team in that competition. Transfers and unused bench registrations cannot move historical awards to a new team.
- Records Player has Performance/Awards controls, eight aggregate metrics and a detailed award selector, with season, competition, historical team and position filters. Total Honors excludes runners-up. The catalog includes monthly, competition/season MVP, scorer/assister, GK, XI, round and final metrics.
- Records Team has Honors & History: total/per-competition titles and runners-up, Cup/Champions final appearances, best League/Cup/Champions finishes, and League season points/wins/goal difference. Replays do not duplicate final appearances; best-season rows display their season.
- Champions uses the existing League SegmentedControl, period selector, AwardBestXI and Pitch, including rating colors, photos, emblems, blue MVP/star treatment and player navigation. Completed round status and final MVP are displayed separately.
- Ordinary defensive/GK rates include every valid role interval; 60-minute and 30% requirements are removed. Zero denominators remain unavailable. REV13 formulas, competition operation, saved football data, import/export and sync modules remain unchanged.
- Records View All reuses the existing app-content virtual list; same-length scope changes remeasure rows. Official season models and award Records are cached by source identities and rating/award policy revisions.
